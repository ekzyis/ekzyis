**XP points gained so far: `+22 (+3 from yesterday)`**

Nothing special to mention today. Just learned that [using LND subscriptions in the
backend](https://github.com/stackernews/stacker.news/pull/726) for faster invoice confirmation might
not be that hard. Currently, we poll every 5 seconds (since that's very easy to do) to check if an
invoice was paid. If the invoice is older than 5 minutes, than we only poll every 60 seconds.

But with LND subscriptions, we're able to confirm invoices basically in real time. We only need to
also use subscriptions in the frontend. Currently, we poll every second in the frontend.

This is required for a good UX when we release our [first iteration on self-custodial
wallets](https://github.com/stackernews/stacker.news/pull/715) using [Bitcoin
Connect](https://github.com/getAlby/bitcoin-connect/tree/master). We really want to stay or get as
close to the current UX regarding zaps.
