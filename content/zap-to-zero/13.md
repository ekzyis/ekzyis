Become aware of your surroundings and how you feel [like described in this
video](https://youtu.be/l5NK8zdIK-g?t=840)

> [...] I think that was my first mistake: Meditating isn't nothing. It's becoming extremely aware
> of your self; of where you are; of the things around you. I am not a meditation professional _but_
> — for me, personally, I really liked the concept of open meditation: instead of focusing on
> something and trying to get all the thoughts out of your head; open meditation was trying to
> absorb everything around you. Notice everything. Every sound, every feeling, how does the floor
> feel under your feet. How does the chair feel under you. There is a person over there, there is a
> door over there. Just taking in everything and realizing where you are. [...]
